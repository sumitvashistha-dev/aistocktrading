Privacy Policy

AI Stock Trading (StockAing) · Effective date: October 22, 2026

AI Stock Trading (StockAing) is an app by Sumit Vashistha (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app stores, who processes it, and how to ask us to delete it.

Accounts

The current version has no user accounts. The app gives your phone an anonymous device ID, kept in the phone’s secure storage. The data below is stored against that device ID, not against your name or email.

Data we store

We store the following, keyed to your anonymous device ID:

  • Watchlist tickers.
  • Your push notification token and your alert and notification settings.
  • In-app usage events (analytics).
  • Feedback text you type in the app.
  • AI chat questions you send. Chat questions are answered by OpenAI, the AI provider our backend uses.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.

Advertising and sharing

There are no ads in the app. We do not share data for advertising or tracking.

We share data only with providers that process it on our behalf: hosting and database providers, OpenAI (for AI chat), RevenueCat (for subscriptions), market data and push delivery providers. We disclose data only where the law requires it.

Market data

Stock prices and market data come from Polygon (Massive). Market data is delayed 15 minutes.

Hosting and security

Our backend runs at weekendhangouts.com. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored with a hosted database provider that encrypts data at rest.

Deleting your data

Email indishadestore@gmail.com with your request, and we will remove the data stored for your device. We respond to access and deletion requests within 30 days.

Children

AI Stock Trading is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Not financial advice

AI Stock Trading is for information only and is not financial advice. Scores and rankings are generated by AI models, may be incomplete, delayed or wrong, and do not guarantee future results.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is material, we will also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
